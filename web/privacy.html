Privacy Policy

NuStreak · Last updated 21 November 2026

NuStreak is a food and nutrition tracking app: you log meals (by photo, search, barcode or by hand), see your calories and nutrients, keep a streak, and share with friends. This policy explains what we collect, why, who else handles it, and the choices you have. It is written in plain language on purpose.

1. Who we are

NuStreak is operated from Uganda. For privacy questions or requests, write to 18vicu@gmail.com. We are the controller of your personal data for the purposes of Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, and, for people in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the equivalent data protection laws there.

2. Who can use NuStreak

NuStreak is for adults. You must be 18 or older. We ask for your date of birth when you set up your profile and do not create calorie plans for anyone under 18. If you believe a child is using the app, tell us and we will delete the account.

4. Who else handles your data

We use these services to run NuStreak. They process data on our behalf, under their own terms and security measures:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and private file storage.
  • Render and Vercel: they host our server and our website.
  • Google: (a) Google Sign-In, if you choose it; (b) the Gemini API, which analyses meal photos and writes the AI coach's and insights' text; (c) web fonts for the website, which your browser may load from Google Fonts.
  • USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts: public food databases. Our server sends them the food name or barcode you searched for, never your account details.
  • Browser and phone notification services (for example those run by Google, Apple or Mozilla): they deliver notifications to your device.

Because these services run in different countries, your data may be processed outside Uganda and outside your own country.

5. What is sent to Google's Gemini

  • Meal scans: the photo, plus the note and region/language hint if you add them.
  • AI coach: your message, and a short summary built on our server: your calorie and protein targets and totals for the day, and the titles and nutrition numbers of your saved recipes. We do not send your name, email, username, picture or friends.
  • Insights: a summary of what you logged recently.

We do not store scan photos; they are used to get the result and then discarded. Under the terms that apply to the free version of the Gemini API, Google may use what is sent to improve its products and may have it reviewed by people, with the content separated from your account. If we move to a paid version, Google's paid terms apply instead, which do not allow that. Either way, please do not put anything in a photo, note or coach message that you would not want a service provider to see (for example faces of other people, documents or medical papers).

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account data, logs, recipes and messages are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Bites and stories are available for 24 hours and are then removed by routine clean-up.
  • You can clear your AI coach conversation at any time. Insights are refreshed from your logs and replaced.
  • When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), we delete your profile and everything attached to it, including your photos in storage, and your sign-in. Copies in our service providers' backups can remain for a limited period before they are overwritten. Reports about content you sent can be kept for as long as needed to protect other people. Messages you sent are deleted with your account.

7. Your choices and rights

You can see and change your profile, targets and food logs in the app, unsend your messages, remove your photos, clear the coach, turn notifications off, and delete your account in Settings. You can also ask us, at 18vicu@gmail.com, to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data, or correct it;
  • delete it, restrict how we use it, or stop using it for something;
  • explain how your data is used.

We will answer within a reasonable time (within one month where the law of your country says so). If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the regulator: in Uganda, the Personal Data Protection Office (under NITA-U); in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, your local data protection authority.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our database uses row-level security so that accounts only reach their own data, photos are kept in private storage and shown through short-lived links, and the AI features are limited per person. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

9. Not medical advice

NuStreak, its calorie targets, the AI coach and its insights give general information and estimates, not medical advice. Food photos are estimated by AI and can be wrong. If you have a health condition, are pregnant, or have or may have an eating disorder, talk to a doctor or dietitian. If you feel unwell or in distress, contact a medical professional or your local emergency number.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app. Questions are always welcome at 18vicu@gmail.com.
